Ember & Olive is a modern casual dining restaurant located in Johannesburg, South Africa. We serve a mix of international dishes inspired by Mediterranean, American, and contemporary global cuisine.

We focus on fresh ingredients, wood-fired cooking, and a relaxed atmosphere perfect for families, friends, and business lunches.
